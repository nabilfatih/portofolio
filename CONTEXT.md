# Portfolio domain

- **Case study**: A public account of one delivered product or system. It explains the problem, Nabil's ownership, the design, and the evidence that can be shared without exposing private employer or customer information.
- **Experience**: A chronological record of professional work and education. It states where the work happened and links to related case studies when a public account exists.
- **Work hub**: The page that connects case studies with the experience record. It helps a reader understand both engineering breadth and career history.
- **Collaboration offer**: The public description of problems Nabil can own for a client or partner. Case studies provide evidence for that offer.
- **Agent document**: A text equivalent of a public portfolio page for agents and other non-visual readers. It must preserve the same claims and links as the visual page.
